## Every Biome Seed
```

```

A seed where literally every biome and structure appears within just a 1000 block radius from coordinates 0,0. A statistically ridiculous combination that many players thought was nearly impossible. From villages and temples to oceans, jungles, snow biomes, and more, everything is compressed into a single area of the map.
